Welcome!

From now on, links to ČSFD on any website show the film's rating.

How it works

  • Plain-text ČSFD URLs become clickable links.
  • Ratings of the first 10 films on a page appear right away.
  • For other films, hover the link for a moment.

What the colours mean

  • 78 % 70 % and more
  • 54 % 30–69 %
  • 23 % less than 30 %
  • – % No rating yet

You can turn it off for a site in the toolbar icon, or switch to hover cards in the settings.

Unofficial extension, not affiliated with ČSFD.
